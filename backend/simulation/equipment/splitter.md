# Splitter (`splitter`)

## 1. Functional Overview

Junction that splits one inlet into two outlets at equal pressure (no internal friction); the inflow equals the sum of the outflows.

> Short description. The full description per the equipment documentation standard is on the ROADMAP ("Detailed Equipment Documentation").
