## [0.2.2]

### Fixes
- **Reducer inspector crash**: selecting a reducer or expander with a connected line no longer crashes the app. The inspector used icons it did not import.
- **Lint guard**: ESLint now reports JSX components that are used without being imported (`react/jsx-no-undef`).
